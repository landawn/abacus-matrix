1. OVERALL ASSESSMENT
A clean, faithful mirror of the IntMatrix baseline with correctly specialized floating-point behavior.
Across the public surface the families are internally consistent: get/set raw access -> AIOOBE; value*
-> OptionalDouble after checkRowColumnIndex (IOOBE); diagonal/row/column/update/transform/arithmetic/stream
families match their counterparts in naming, parameter order, return types, and validation. NaN/Infinity/-0.0
semantics are unusually well documented (equals, replaceIf, add/subtract/matmul, mutateFlattened sort ordering,
from(float[]), the toXxxMatrix narrowers, and println all carry accurate notes; the doubleToRawLongBits!=0
default-value fast-path in resize/extend is correct). Conversions are complete and consistent (toIntMatrix
2909, toLongMatrix 2964, toFloatMatrix 3013 = the three main numeric types other than double; all narrowing,
all documented). The only actionable items are the missing mapToFloat (asymmetric vs the existing
toFloatMatrix) and the family-wide from()/reductions gaps.

CROSS-CLASS NOTES
- Conversions: only narrowing toInt/toLong/toFloat (correct — double is widest; the "to {int,long,float,double}
  except self" convention holds). No widening targets exist, correctly.
- mapToInt/mapToLong present (mirrors LongMatrix's narrowing mapTo); mapToFloat is the lone gap (family-wide).
- from differs from IntMatrix (int/long/float vs char/byte/short) as expected since from widens toward the
  class's own type; the lattice gap is shared with Long/Float.
- range/rangeClosed correctly absent (integer-only).
