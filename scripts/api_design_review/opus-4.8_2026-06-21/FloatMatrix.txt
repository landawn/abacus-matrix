API Design Review — FloatMatrix
MODEL opus-4.8 | DATE 2026-06-21 | file: src/main/java/com/landawn/abacus/matrix/FloatMatrix.java (3829 lines)
All findings verified against source. FloatMatrix is the primitive class with the MOST coverage gaps.

1. OVERALL ASSESSMENT
The instance-level surface (get/set, value*, diagonals, update*/replaceIf/map, transforms, arithmetic,
streams, boxed/unbox, equals/hashCode) faithfully mirrors the family, and IEEE-754 semantics are handled
responsibly: equals/hashCode use floatToIntBits/deepEquals (NaN==NaN, +0.0f != -0.0f) with clear docs;
replaceIf, add/subtract/matmul, mutateAsFlat and println carry accurate NaN/Infinity notes; the narrowing
toIntMatrix/toLongMatrix document saturation and NaN->0; and the -0.0f default-value fast-path in
resize/extend (Float.floatToRawIntBits(defaultValue) != 0) is correct. Conversions are complete and
consistent (toDouble 2711, toInt 2748, toLong 2804 = the three main numeric types other than float). The
actionable items are all coverage gaps — and FloatMatrix is the clear outlier on two of them: it has ZERO
primitive mapTo* methods, and Matrices has NO static zip family for it.

5. MISSING METHODS

Primitive mapToInt / mapToLong / mapToDouble | FloatMatrix.java:1323 (only mapToObj exists) | Confidence High |
recommend: add mapToInt(FloatToIntFunction), mapToLong(FloatToLongFunction), mapToDouble(FloatToDoubleFunction).
  > public <R, E extends Exception> Matrix<R> mapToObj(final Throwables.FloatFunction<? extends R, E> mapper, final Class<R> targetElementType)
  FloatMatrix has ZERO primitive-to-primitive mapTo methods, while its numeric peers each have two
  (IntMatrix: mapToLong/mapToDouble; LongMatrix: mapToInt/mapToDouble; DoubleMatrix: mapToInt/mapToLong).
  toIntMatrix/toLongMatrix/toDoubleMatrix already exist but are blanket casts, NOT substitutes for an
  arbitrary per-element function. This is the clearest mapTo* outlier in the family.
